Admission

The Ann Arbor Art Museum welcomes local residents, University of Michigan students, visiting scholars, families, tourists, and school groups. Because Ann Arbor has a busy calendar of university events,football weekends, festivals, conferences, and downtown activities, visitors are encouraged to check museum hours before arriving.

Admission fees support exhibitions, conservation, public programs, student learning opportunities, accessibility services, and partnerships with Ann Arbor schools and community organizations.

Free Admission Days

The museum offers free general admission on the first Sunday of every month. These days are designed to welcome Ann Arbor residents, students, families, and visitors who want to explore local culture without an admission cost.

Free admission days often include short gallery talks, family activity stations, and introductions to exhibits about Ann Arbor neighborhoods, local artists, and the Huron River.

Membership Benefits

Museum members receive free general admission, early access to select exhibitions, invitations to preview events, and discounts in the museum store. Memberships help support Ann Arbor-focused exhibitions, student programs, artist talks, and preservation of local cultural materials.

  1. Choose a membership level.
  2. Complete the online or in-person registration form.
  3. Show your membership card when you arrive.

Group Visits and School Programs

Groups of ten or more visitors should contact the museum in advance.Group reservations help staff prepare gallery guides, accessibility accommodations, and gathering spaces.

School groups can request tours connected to Ann Arbor history, public art, environmental studies, visual analysis, or careers in museums and cultural organizations.

Group leaders should arrive at least fifteen minutes early to check in before the scheduled visit.
